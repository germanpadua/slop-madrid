# Palacio de Cibeles: frontage and crown correction

The palace previously placed its monumental entrance and two towers along an axis-aligned west wall. The municipal façade survey identifies that frontage as **northwest**. The corrected entrance and tower group follows the northwest diagonal between the two small lobes in the existing OSM landmark ring. The source footprint remains unchanged; there is no overall rescaling.

Sources inspected on 2026-10-29:

- [Ayuntamiento: building survey, PDF page 26 (printed page 20)](https://sede.madrid.es/UnidadesDescentralizadas/PerfilContratante/PC_Areas/PC_MAmbienteMov/2017/D.G.%20Gesti%C3%B3n%20del%20Agua%20y%20Zonas%20Verdes/ficheros/01362%20Anejo%20I%20al%20PPT.pdf): labels the northwest façade and describes pentagonal towers and a 70 m octagonal cimborrio.
- [Municipal heritage description](https://patrimonioypaisaje.madrid.es/portales/monumenta/es/Monumentos-y-Edificios-Singulares/Edificios-singulares/Palacio-de-Comunicaciones-Palacio-de-Cibeles-Ayuntamiento-de-Madrid-/?vgnextchannel=83bc3cb702aa4510VgnVCM1000008a4a900aRCRD&vgnextfmt=default&vgnextoid=4128f7d9560a4510f7d9560a45102e085a0aRCRD): confirms the six-storey building, pentagonal towers, and 70 m clock cimborrio above the crossing.
- [Official Madrid tourism photo gallery](https://www.esmadrid.com/informacion-turistica/palacio-cibeles), frontal photograph `palacio_de_cibeles_3.jpg`: visibly open dark tower galleries, tall narrow window bays, central arched entrance and pinnacled crowns. Broad conical slate roofs in the old model do not match this reference. The photograph was inspected locally; it is not copied into the repository and no metric dimensions were inferred from it.

The shafts now stop below their gallery bands, which were previously hidden inside solid cylinders. Narrow windows follow the tapered pentagonal faces. Simplified balustrade rims and slender pinnacles replace the broad cone roofs. The lower clock drum starts at the 28 m main-body roof, keeping the northwest tower attached even where its anchor is outside the recessed upper tier. The central clock stage sits below its tall upper gallery, as in the official frontal photograph. Its four clock disks and dials face radially outward, and the principal face turns toward the northwest entrance. The central crown reaches the documented 70 m, and the existing full-site collision box has the same maximum height. QA group names: `CibelesNorthwestFrontage`, `CibelesNorthwestEntrance`, `CibelesNorthTower`, `CibelesSouthTower`, `CibelesClockTower`.

Limits: source-lobe bounding-box centres provide illustrative tower anchors, not surveyed tower centres. Shaft heights/radii, window dimensions, relief, central tower setback and crown subdivisions remain low-poly approximations. The existing body tiers are retained. The rear portico remains an unresolved approximation and is no longer incorrectly described as the Paseo del Prado frontage. Courtyards, sculpture, precise bays and overlapping generic buildings require separate source-backed work. This correction does not establish architectural parity or metric accuracy.

Validation: `node tests/cibeles-architecture.test.cjs` passes 4/4. Assertions cover northwest orientation toward the fountain, diagonal tower placement, exposed galleries, visible shaft windows, unchanged source footprint, slender crowns 70 m central apex/collider, vertical continuity from the main roof through the lower drum into the clock stage, clock-below-gallery stacking and the transformed radial normals of all four clock faces/dials. Hardware street and model captures were inspected: see 2026-10-29-cibeles/README.md for the comparison and remaining fidelity problems.
